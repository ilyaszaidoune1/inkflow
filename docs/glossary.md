# Glossary

## Article

A piece of content generated and managed by InkFlow.

---

## Opportunity

A potential topic that may become an article.

---

## Workflow

A sequence of stages executed to transform an opportunity into published content.

---

## Workflow Stage

A single step within a workflow, such as Research or Publishing.

---

## AI Provider

An external service capable of generating content, such as OpenAI.

---

## Publishing Platform

A destination where completed articles are published.

Examples include WordPress, Ghost, Medium, or custom APIs.

---

## Repository

The Git repository containing the InkFlow source code and documentation.

---

## Service

A component responsible for a single business capability.

Examples include the Research Service or Publishing Service.

---

## Provider

A component responsible for communicating with an external system.

Examples include AI providers or publishing providers.

---

## Workflow Engine

The component responsible for coordinating workflow execution.
